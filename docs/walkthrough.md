# The 60-second walkthrough

The last open item from the build order. Record it on a phone-sized window and a laptop, in that order, since the phone flow is what the agent feedback asked for.

**Before recording**
- Turn on Do Not Disturb on the Mac and the phone. The agent who reviewed the app saw notifications over the top of the screen in an earlier demo.
- Open https://listing-check.vercel.app in a clean window, light mode, and press Clear so the autosave offer does not appear.
- Hide bookmarks and extensions. The Next.js "N" badge is dev-only and does not appear on the live site.

**Script, about 60 seconds**

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Empty form, "Nothing to check yet" | "Listing Check reads a draft rental listing before it goes live." |
| 0:05 | Tap Example draft | "Here is a typical rushed draft: caps title, two-line description, no floor, three photos. It scores 70." |
| 0:15 | Check tab on the phone, or the right column | "Every missing field is listed with what to add. The price check compares rent per square metre with similar listings, and names them." |
| 0:25 | Fix the floor, watch the +8 | "Fix a field and the score moves straight away." |
| 0:32 | Use the usual terms | "Lease terms are one tap, and they become facts the rewrite may state." |
| 0:38 | Rewrite with AI, review overlay | "The rewrite writes English and Thai from the draft alone. Anything it adds that was not in the draft is highlighted, and nothing is applied until you accept it." |
| 0:50 | Preview as a listing | "The preview shows the draft as a portal page, with the gaps still marked." |
| 0:56 | Export, Copy as text | "Then copy it out, or export JSON for your system." |
